📋 教学方案

🎯 教学目标

语言目标（词汇）field, grass, trees, forest, wind, leaves, rock, lake, river, mountain, ground, water 语言目标（句型）What is there? / Where is the…? / How do…feel? / There is a… 瑜伽体式Upward Salute → Tree Pose → Side Reach → Standing Balance → Seated Spinal Twist → Mountain Pose → Butterfly Pose → Savasana 幸福力目标投入（沉浸式身体探索）、成就（登顶俯瞰）、积极情绪（平静与感恩） 时长约 15–18 分钟 年龄7–10 岁

📖 教学步骤

阶段一：导入（P1–P2）｜约 2–3 分钟

P1 · 声音悬念 + 引出主题 开场
😊 好奇→神秘→微笑🧘 歪头→手指耳朵→微笑
(Play forest sounds 3 sec—birds, rustling leaves—pause, tilt head, confused) "Wait… (pause 2s) did you hear that? (Play 2s more, pause) There it is again! (Lower voice, look around) That's not a classroom sound. I think… it's calling us to take a walk. (Look up at cover, smile) Welcome. Today we're going to explore a peaceful place—with our bodies. Are you ready?"
P2 · 跟着声音走
😊 好奇、期待🧘 闭眼深呼吸→睁眼→环视
(Play white noise low volume, walk slowly, feet gently stepping) "(Close eyes, deep breath, then slowly open) Mmm… can you feel that? (Look down, lightly step) Green and soft grass under your feet. (Look around, eyes wide) Where are we? Let's find out together…"

阶段二：核心探索（P3–P9）｜约 10–12 分钟

P3 · 仰望巨树 Upward Salute
😊 惊叹、好奇🧘 双臂上举，身体向上伸展
(Look up, arms slowly rising) "The trees here are SO tall! Look at those giant trees… reaching up to the sky! Can you reach up like them? Stretch your arms to the sky… higher… (Body stretching upward) How tall can YOU grow? What do you see way up there?"

→ 如果学生沉默：教师自己先回答（I see a bird! A cloud!），重新问"What else?"
→ 如果学生说了中文：用英文重复 + 点头微笑
P4 · 成为森林中的树 Tree Pose
😊 好奇、鼓励🧘 单腿站立，手臂展开如树枝
(Look around, amazed) "Look around this forest—every tree is different! That one is tall and thin… (Point) that one is short and round… (Stand firm) Pick ONE tree. Become that tree! (Lift one foot, arms out) Are your branches reaching up high, or stretching wide?"
P5 · 接飘落的树叶 Side Reach
😊 兴奋、活泼🧘 站姿左右交替侧伸展
(Body swaying, arms stretching) "Whoosh! The wind is blowing! Leaves are falling everywhere! Quick—reach left! (Body stretches left) Now reach right! (Body stretches right) Left… right… (Alternating) Can you catch one before it touches the ground?"
P6 · 岩石上找平衡 Standing Balance
😊 专注、兴奋🧘 单腿站立，双臂展开
(Stand on one foot, arms out) "There's a big rock in the middle of the field. Let's step onto it! One foot first… now the other… (Find balance) Can you balance like a mountain goat? Arms out like wings! (Slight wobble) What's under your feet right now?"

→ 如果学生站不稳：It's okay to wobble! Even mountain goats wobble. Touch the wall if you need. Or keep one toe on the ground.
P7 · 扭动如水流 Seated Spinal Twist
😊 惊喜、灵动🧘 坐姿身体左右扭转
(Hand shading eyes, looking far) "Wait—I see something sparkling far away! A lake… and a river! (Sit down slowly, body gently twisting) The water wiggles and wiggles, never stopping. Can you wiggle like the water? (Body twists to one side) Wiggle, wiggle this way… now wiggle the other way… (Twist to other side) Where is YOUR river wiggling to?"
P8 · 到达山顶 Mountain Pose
😊 坚定、平静🧘 坐姿→站姿，双手合十胸前
(Slowly stand up from sitting, hands on ground) "We've been by the water… now let's climb up! (Slowly stand straight) One step… another step… (Stand tall, hands at heart) Standing tall as the mountain. From up here… (Deep breath) …I can see everything we've explored. How does it feel?"

→ 如果学生说"累"：Me too. My legs feel the climbing. That means we really went somewhere.
→ 如果学生说"开心"：Happy. Me too. Standing on top of the world.
P9 · 坐下来，感受大地 Butterfly Pose
😊 温柔、平静🧘 坐姿，脚底相对，膝盖轻扇
(Sit down, softly) "We've explored so much—the trees, the wind, the rocks, the water. Our legs are tired now. Let's sit down together. (Soles of feet together) What if your knees were butterfly wings? Softly flap them… up and down. (Knees gently flap) Can you feel the ground holding you? Right here, right now."

阶段三：回归放松（P10–P11）｜约 3–4 分钟

P10 · 回顾旅程，躺下休息 Savasana
😊 温柔、平静🧘 仰卧，四肢放松，闭眼
(Lie down, softly, slow down) "We've explored so much—the grass, the trees, the leaves, the rock, the water, and the mountain. It's time to rest now. Lie down… close your eyes… (Deep breath) Your legs remember the walking, the balancing, the wiggling. Your arms remember the reaching. Let it all go. (Silence 10–15 seconds)"
P11 · 感谢与告别
😊 温暖、平静、感恩🧘 双手合十，微笑，轻声
(Stay silent 10–15s, then softly, smile) "Thank you for walking with me today. The trees, the wind, the lake, the mountain… they're all inside you now. Take that peaceful feeling with you. See you next time. (Slowly sit up, smile and wait) When you're ready, slowly open your eyes… wiggle your fingers and toes… and sit up."

💡 教学提示

  1. 英文是工具，不是目标 — 学生用中文回应完全 OK，教师用英文重复 + 点头微笑即可。
  2. 体式不求标准 — 每个孩子的身体条件不同，观察但不评判。
  3. 不比较，不竞赛 — 不要说"看看谁站得最稳"，每个孩子的身体表达都是对的。
  4. P7→P8 过渡注意 — 唯一从坐姿回到站姿的节点，教师先自己慢慢站起，给学生时间。
  5. P9→P10 过渡注意 — 从 Butterfly Pose 到 Savasana，教师先说"Time to rest"，自然躺下。
  6. P10 放松环节不跳过 — 即使时间紧，至少保留 1 分钟，这是整个活动的情感收束。
  7. P11 结束不拖沓 — 告别语温暖收尾，不需要额外总结。
