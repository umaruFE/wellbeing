# The Sky in Me — 教学方案 & 歌曲编唱屋教学引导

## 基础信息

| 项目 | 内容 |
|------|------|
| 歌曲名称 | The Sky in Me |
| 旋律来源 | You're My Sunshine |
| 适配年龄 | 7-9 岁 |
| 英文水平 | 初级（Beginner） |
| 总时长 | 约 30 分钟 |
| 幸福力主题 | 情绪认知与表达 |
| PERMA 连接 | P 情绪被歌唱的积极体验 / E 音乐与情绪探索的沉浸 / R 分享内心感受的联结 / M 每种情绪都有意义 / A 完成属于自己的情绪之歌 |

## 语言目标

| 类型 | 内容 |
|------|------|
| 核心词汇 | happy, excited, sad, mad, angry, tired, bored, shy, calm（9 个情绪词） |
| 句型结构 | When I am ___, I want to ___. / When I feel ___, I ___. |
| 填词难度 | 低（仅填单词，6 个空格，9 词 Word Bank） |

---

## 五步活动流程（含英文讲稿）

---

### 第一步：情绪感知导入（5 分钟）

**目标**：激活情绪词汇背景知识，建立情绪-身体-声音的连接

#### 讲稿 & 引导

> 🎵 *[播放 You're My Sunshine 伴奏，音量 20%，作为背景音乐]*

> **Teacher**：*[轻声，放慢语速，眼神柔和扫视全班]*
>
> "Everyone, find a comfortable position. You can sit on your chair, or on the floor. Close your eyes. Take a deep breath in... and out."
>
> *[示范：闭眼，手放在腹部，慢吸慢呼。停顿 5 秒]*
>
> "Now, I want you to think about TODAY. How did you feel when you woke up this morning? *[停顿 5 秒]* How did you feel when you came to school? *[停顿 5 秒]* How do you feel right now, in this moment?"
>
> *[停顿 8 秒]*
>
> "Feelings come and go. They change like the sky. Sometimes sunny, sometimes cloudy, sometimes rainy. Every feeling is okay. Every feeling belongs."
>
> *[停顿 5 秒]*
>
> "When you're ready, slowly open your eyes."

> **Teacher**：*[恢复正常音量，面带微笑，双手张开]* "Welcome back! Let me ask you — how do you feel right now? Just one word. Raise your hand."

> **Student A**："Happy."
>
> **Teacher**：*[双手捧脸，做大大笑脸，眼睛眯起来]* "Happy! A big, sunny happy! *[在白板上写 "happy"，画一个笑脸]* Thank you!"

> **Student B**："Tired."
>
> **Teacher**：*[肩膀耷拉，眼皮下垂，夸张打哈欠]* "Tired. Yes, sometimes we feel tired. *[写 "tired"，画困倦表情]* That's okay too."

> **Student C**："Excited!"
>
> **Teacher**：*[双手握拳在胸前，身体微微跳起]* "Excited! Like you have butterflies in your stomach! *[写 "excited"，画星星眼]*"

> *[收集 3-5 个学生分享，将情绪词写在白板上。如果学生用中文说，引导师转成英文并写在白板上，同时微笑点头鼓励。]*

> **Teacher**：*[指着白板上的情绪词]* "Look at all these feelings! Happy, tired, excited... all different. And ALL of them are okay. Feelings are like the sky — *[手指向上]* — they change, and every sky is beautiful."

**TPR 动作指南**：

| 情绪 | 动作 | 表情 |
|------|------|------|
| happy | 双手捧脸，嘴角上扬 | 眼睛眯起来 |
| excited | 双手握拳胸前，身体微跳 | 眼睛睁大，星星眼 |
| sad | 双手下垂，肩膀耷拉 | 嘴角向下 |
| angry | 双手握拳，眉头紧皱 | 嘴巴紧闭 |
| tired | 肩膀耷拉，眼皮下垂 | 打哈欠 |
| bored | 单手托腮，眼神放空 | 面无表情 |
| shy | 双手半遮脸，微微低头 | 眼睛从指缝看 |
| calm | 双手放在胸口，慢呼吸 | 眼睛微闭，微笑 |

**引导师备忘**：
- 第一步全程用柔和的"冥想引导"语气，不要急促
- 闭眼环节如果学生害羞，允许睁眼但保持安静
- 学生分享时，每听到一个情绪词就做对应动作，强化身体记忆
- 如果学生说不出英文，微笑着说："That's okay! Let me help you. You feel... happy?"
- 核心信息反复强化：每一种情绪都是可以的，情绪像天空一样变化

---

### 第二步：词汇探索（5 分钟）

**目标**：认识 9 个情绪词，建立词-表情-动作-声音四重连接

#### 讲稿 & 引导

> **Teacher**：*[点击 HTML 页面 🔍 按钮，全屏展示 Word Bank]*
>
> "Now let's meet our feeling words! We have NINE feelings today. Look at the screen. Each feeling has a face. I'll say the word, you repeat after me. AND do the face! Ready?"

> **Teacher**：*[指着 happy 卡片，双手捧脸，做最大笑脸]* "Happy! Hap-py! *[夸张地发两个音节]*"
>
> **All students**："Happy!" *[也做笑脸]*
>
> **Teacher**："Again! Happy!"
>
> **All students**："Happy!"

> **Teacher**：*[指着 excited 卡片，握拳胸前，身体微跳]* "Excited! Ex-ci-ted! Three syllables! *[拍三下手]* Ex-ci-ted!"
>
> **All students**："Excited!"

> **Teacher**：*[指着 sad 卡片，双手下垂，嘴角向下]* "Sad! Just one little word — sad. *[叹气]*"
>
> **All students**："Sad."

> **Teacher**：*[指着 mad 卡片，双手握拳，眉头紧皱]* "Mad! M-A-D. When you're mad, everything feels HOT! *[用手扇风]*"
>
> **All students**："Mad!"

> **Teacher**：*[指着 angry 卡片，握拳，身体绷紧]* "Angry! An-gry! Like a volcano! *[双手从胸前向上爆发]*"
>
> **All students**："Angry!"

> **Teacher**：*[指着 tired 卡片，肩膀耷拉，打哈欠]* "Tired! Ti-red. Two syllables. *[拍两下]* Ti-red. When you're tired, your body is HEAVY."
>
> **All students**："Tired."

> **Teacher**：*[指着 bored 卡片，单手托腮，眼神放空]* "Bored! One syllable — bored. When you're bored, everything is BLAH. *[摊手]*"
>
> **All students**："Bored."

> **Teacher**：*[指着 shy 卡片，双手半遮脸，从指缝看]* "Shy! Shy. When you're shy, you want to hide. *[缩肩膀]* But it's okay to be shy."
>
> **All students**："Shy."

> **Teacher**：*[指着 calm 卡片，双手放在胸口，慢呼吸]* "Calm! One syllable — calm. When you're calm, your body is quiet. *[闭眼，深呼吸]*"
>
> **All students**："Calm."

> **Teacher**：*[拍手两下，切换模式，表情变得兴奋]* "Great! Now — feeling game! I do the face, YOU say the word. Fast! Ready?"

> *[快速做表情：大笑脸 → 皱眉握拳 → 打哈欠 → 托腮放空 → 握拳跳起 → 半遮脸 → 闭眼慢呼吸 → 双手下垂]*
>
> **All students**：*[此起彼伏]* "Happy! Angry! Tired! Bored! Excited! Shy! Calm! Sad!"

> **Teacher**：*[加快速度，故意做错一两个，让学生纠正]* "Wait, was that happy? *[做困倦表情]*"
>
> **Students**："NO! Tired!"
>
> **Teacher**：*[大笑]* "You got me! You are SO good at this!"

**情绪词-动作-声音速查表**：

| 情绪词 | 动作 | 声音 |
|--------|------|------|
| happy | 双手捧脸，大大笑脸 | 轻快的 "yay!" |
| excited | 握拳胸前，身体微跳 | 急促的 "woo-hoo!" |
| sad | 双手下垂，肩膀耷拉 | 缓慢的 "oh..." |
| mad | 握拳，眉头紧皱 | 短促的 "hmph!" |
| angry | 握拳，身体绷紧 | 爆发的 "grrr!" |
| tired | 肩膀耷拉，打哈欠 | 沉重的 "ugh..." |
| bored | 单手托腮，眼神放空 | 无精打采的 "meh..." |
| shy | 双手半遮脸，缩肩 | 微弱的 "um..." |
| calm | 手放胸口，慢呼吸 | 轻柔的 "shh..." |

**发音重点**：

| 词汇 | 发音重点 | 技巧 |
|------|---------|------|
| excited | /ɪkˈsaɪ.tɪd/ | 三个音节，重音在第二音节，不要读成 "excited" |
| angry | /ˈæŋ.ɡri/ | /æ/ 嘴巴张大，不要读成 "angly" |
| tired | /taɪərd/ | 两个音节，不要读成 "tired"（一个音节） |
| bored | /bɔːrd/ | 长音 /ɔːr/，不要读成 "bore" |
| calm | /kɑːm/ | L 不发音！不要读成 "calm" 带 L |

**引导师备忘**：
- 每个词做表情时保持 2 秒，让学生看清并模仿
- 表情游戏阶段逐渐加速，制造"来不及想只能脱口而出"的效果
- 故意做错表情让学生纠正，增加趣味性和参与感
- 发音不准时不在本环节单独纠正，下一轮集体朗读时自己大声示范
- 核心信息：mad 和 angry 的区别不强求，学生能说出一个即可

### 第三步：歌曲创编挑战（8 分钟）

**目标**：在「You're My Sunshine」旋律中，将情绪词填入歌词，理解"情绪像天空一样变化"的隐喻，创建属于自己的情绪之歌

#### 3.1 认识歌曲结构（2 分钟）

> **Teacher**：*[关闭全屏 Word Bank，回到 HTML 主页面，指着歌词]* "Now, look at our song. This melody is 'You're My Sunshine.' You might know it. *[哼唱前两句]* 'You are my sunshine, my only sunshine...'"

> **Teacher**："But today, we have NEW words. OUR words. About OUR feelings. Let's read it together first. I'll read, you follow."

> **Teacher**：*[指着第一行，缓慢朗读，手指跟着文字移动]* "When I am... *[指着空格]* hmm... something... I want to sing. *[耸肩，表示这是空]*

> *[继续逐行朗读，空格处用"hmm"代替，配合耸肩]*

> **Teacher**："When I am hmm, I want to sing. When I am hmm, I dance and swing. When I feel hmm, I close my eyes. Oh, my feelings change like the sky."

> **Teacher**：*[翻到第二段]* "When I am hmm, I jump up high. When I am hmm, I want to cry. When I feel hmm, I breathe in deep. Oh, my feelings, mine to keep."

> **Teacher**："See the last line of each part? 'My feelings change like the sky.' 'My feelings, mine to keep.' These are the MOST important lines. They tell us: feelings change, and YOUR feelings belong to YOU."

#### 3.2 分组创编（3 分钟）

> **Teacher**：*[两人一组]* "Now, you and your partner — open the web page. You will see the Word Bank on the right side. NINE feeling words. Drag the words to the blanks. Make YOUR version of the song!"

> **Teacher**："Here's the rule: there are NO wrong answers. Choose the feelings that feel TRUE to YOU. When you want to sing — are you happy? Excited? Calm? When you want to cry — are you sad? Angry? Tired? It's YOUR song, YOUR feelings."

> **Teacher**："Three minutes! Work together! Talk to each other! Go!"

> *[学生在平板上拖拽填词，引导师巡视，弯腰观察每组进度，不直接给答案，而是好奇提问]*

> **Teacher**：*[巡视时对某组]* "Oh, you put 'angry' for 'I want to sing'! That's interesting! Tell me — do you sing when you're angry?"

> **Student**："Yes! I sing LOUD!"

> **Teacher**："You sing LOUD when you're angry! That's a real thing! *[竖起大拇指]* Keep it!"

> **Teacher**：*[对另一组]* "I see 'shy' for 'I close my eyes.' Why shy?"

> **Student**："Because when I'm shy... I don't want people to look at me."

> **Teacher**：*[点头，表情温暖]* "So you close your eyes. That makes so much sense. Beautiful choice."

#### 3.3 分享与感受（3 分钟）

> **Teacher**：*[拍手召集]* "Alright! Let's share. Group one — read your first verse to us!"

> **Student A**："When I am happy, I want to sing. When I am excited, I dance and swing. When I feel calm, I close my eyes. Oh, my feelings change like the sky."

> **Teacher**：*[双手放在胸口]* "Wow. Happy, excited, calm. That's a beautiful sky! *[转向全班]* Did anyone choose different feelings for the first verse?"

> **Student B**："We did 'When I am shy, I want to sing'!"

> **Teacher**：*[眼睛睁大]* "Shy! When you're shy, you sing? That's brave! *[轻声]* Sometimes singing helps us feel less shy, doesn't it?"

> **Teacher**："Let's hear the second verse from someone!"

> **Student C**："When I am excited, I jump up high. When I am sad, I want to cry. When I feel tired, I breathe in deep. Oh, my feelings, mine to keep."

> **Teacher**："Excited, sad, tired — all in one verse! That's like a real day! *[手指向上]* The sky changes, right? Sunny, then rainy, then a quiet sunset. Your feelings change too. And that's..."

> **Teacher + Students**："...OKAY!"

> **Teacher**："Look around. Every group has a DIFFERENT song. Different feelings. Different choices. And every song is RIGHT. Because every song is YOURS."

**歌词模板**：

```
🎵 The Sky in Me 🎵

【第一段】
When I am ______, I want to sing.
When I am ______, I dance and swing.
When I feel ______, I close my eyes.
Oh, my feelings change like the sky.

【第二段】
When I am ______, I jump up high.
When I am ______, I want to cry.
When I feel ______, I breathe in deep.
Oh, my feelings, mine to keep.
```

**Word Bank（9 个情绪词）**：happy, excited, sad, mad, angry, tired, bored, shy, calm

**填词引导表**：

| 空格 | 歌词线索 | 好奇提问（不告知答案） | 可能的选择 |
|------|---------|----------------------|-----------|
| 1 | "I want to sing" | 你想唱歌的时候，是什么心情？ | happy / excited / calm / angry |
| 2 | "I dance and swing" | 跳舞摇摆的时候呢？ | happy / excited |
| 3 | "I close my eyes" | 闭上眼睛的时候，你是什么感觉？ | calm / tired / sad / shy |
| 4 | "I jump up high" | 跳起来的时候？ | excited / happy |
| 5 | "I want to cry" | 想哭的时候…… | sad / angry / tired / mad |
| 6 | "I breathe in deep" | 深呼吸的时候，心里是什么感觉？ | calm / angry / tired |

**引导师备忘**：
- 核心原则：没有标准答案！情绪表达是主观的，每个孩子的选择都值得尊重
- 如果孩子选了"出人意料"的词（如 "angry" + "I want to sing"），好奇追问原因，不纠正
- 引导提问时保持开放姿态："Tell me more!" "Why did you choose that?" "That's interesting!"
- 分享环节重点是让每组感受到"我的歌和别人的不一样，但都是对的"
- 通过"天空隐喻"反复强化：情绪变化是正常的，每种情绪都值得被表达

### 第四步：情绪声音配器（5 分钟）

**目标**：通过乐器选择表达对情绪声音的理解，发展情绪-声音-身体的连接

#### 讲稿 & 引导

> **Teacher**：*[向下滚动页面，展示乐器区]* "Now, let's add EMOTION SOUNDS to our song! Every feeling has a sound. Let's find them!"

> **Teacher**：*[拿起手鼓]* "Listen. Happy — *[轻快拍击]* boom-boom, boom-boom! Bright and bouncy!"
>
> **Teacher**：*[拿起砂槌]* "Angry — *[急促摇动]* shh-shh-shh-shh! Fast and strong!"
>
> **Teacher**：*[拿起三角铁]* "Sad — *[轻敲长音]* ting... Soft and slow."
>
> **Teacher**：*[拿起沙锤]* "Calm — *[缓慢摇动]* shh... shh... Like breathing."

> **Teacher**："Now, with your partner, look at YOUR song. Think: what SOUND matches each feeling? Drag TWO instruments to each line. Two minutes! Go!"

> *[学生拖拽乐器到歌词末尾圆圈，引导师巡视]*

> **Teacher**：*[巡视时]* "You chose triangle for 'sad.' Why?"

> **Student**："Because sad is... quiet. Like a tear drop."

> **Teacher**："A tear drop. *[轻敲三角铁]* Ting. That's perfect."

> **Teacher**：*[对另一组]* "Hand drum AND tambourine for 'excited'! That's a party!"

> **Student**："Because excited is LOUD!"

> **Teacher**："Yes! Excited is LOUD and BIG! *[模仿手鼓+铃鼓合奏]* Boom-boom-jingle! I love it!"

**情绪-乐器-声音映射表**：

| 情绪 | 推荐乐器 | 拟声词 | 质感 |
|------|---------|--------|------|
| happy | 手鼓 / 铃鼓 | boom-boom, jingle | 明亮、跳跃 |
| excited | 手鼓+铃鼓 / 响板 | boom-jingle, click-click | 急促、爆发 |
| sad | 三角铁 / 木琴 | ting... doo... | 悠长、低落 |
| mad | 砂槌 | shh-shh-shh | 短促、有力 |
| angry | 砂槌 / 手鼓 | SHH-SHH, BOOM | 激烈、爆发 |
| tired | 沙锤 | shh... shh... | 缓慢、沉重 |
| bored | 三角铁 | ting... ting... | 单调、重复 |
| shy | 三角铁（极轻） | ting... | 微弱、害羞 |
| calm | 沙锤 / 尤克里里 | shh... pluck... | 轻柔、平稳 |

**引导师备忘**：
- 没有标准答案！任何合理的联想都值得鼓励
- 重点不是"对不对"，而是"为什么这样选"
- 用拟声词帮助学生建立声音联想，比直接说"这个适合"更有效
- 如果学生选择困难，提示："Close your eyes. Feel that feeling. What sound do you hear inside?"
- 允许学生用身体打击乐代替乐器（拍手、拍腿、打响指、跺脚）

---

### 第五步：合唱与表达（5 分钟）

**目标**：完整演唱自己创编的情绪之歌，理解"每种情绪都值得被唱出来"

#### 讲稿 & 引导

> **Teacher**：*[拍手，示意全班起立]* "Everyone, stand up! We're going to sing OUR song. YOUR song. Three times!"

> **Teacher**："First time — no voice, just your body. *[手指放在嘴唇上]* Silent singing. Do the feelings with your face and body. Ready?"

> *[播放伴奏，音量 60%。引导师做夸张的表情和动作，全班默唱 + 做表情。]*

> **Teacher**：*[默唱结束，微笑]* "I saw your faces! Happy faces, sad faces, calm faces. Beautiful!"

> **Teacher**："Second time — sing OUT LOUD! All together! Follow me! *[举起双手准备指挥]*"

> *[播放伴奏，引导师指挥节奏，全班齐唱 + 做表情。]*

> **Teacher**：*[齐唱结束，用力鼓掌]* "YES! That was YOUR song! But wait — what about the instruments?"

> **Teacher**："Third time — this is the BIG one! Sing, do the feelings, AND make your instrument sounds! Ready?!"

> *[播放伴奏，全班齐唱 + 做表情 + 模仿乐器声。引导师加入，用嘴模仿乐器声音。]*

> *[歌曲结束，保持安静 3 秒，让余韵停留]*

> **Teacher**：*[轻声，手放在胸口]* "The last line says: 'Oh, my feelings, mine to keep.' What does 'mine to keep' mean?"

> **Student**："It means... my feelings are MINE."

> **Teacher**："Yes. YOUR feelings belong to YOU. Nobody can take them away. Happy, sad, angry, calm — they are all yours. And they are all okay."

> **Teacher**："I want to ask you something. When you sang YOUR song, how did you feel?"

> **Student**："Happy! Because I made it!"

> **Teacher**："You made it. YOUR song. YOUR feelings. YOUR sky. *[手指向上]*"

> **Teacher**："One last thing. Turn to your partner. *[示意]* Say to them: 'Your feelings are okay.'"

> *[学生互相说]*

> **Teacher**："And now say to yourself: 'MY feelings are okay.'"

> *[学生自己说]*

> **Teacher**：*[音量恢复正常，双手张开]* "Today you didn't just LEARN a song. You CREATED a song. A song about YOU. About YOUR feelings. About YOUR sky. Remember: feelings change like the sky — and every sky is beautiful. Thank you, everyone. Give yourselves a big round of applause! *[带头鼓掌]*"

**引导师备忘**：
- 三遍演唱的递进：默唱（感受）→ 齐唱（表达）→ 加乐器声（创造）
- 默唱环节保持安静，只做口型和表情，让旋律和情绪在身体里流动
- 齐唱时不纠正发音或音准，重点是情感连接和参与感
