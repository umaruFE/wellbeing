# Green In My Heart — 教学方案 & 教学引导

## 基础信息

| 项目 | 内容 |
|------|------|
| 歌曲名称 | Green In My Heart |
| 旋律来源 | Edelweiss（雪绒花） |
| 适配年龄 | 10-12 岁 |
| 英文水平 | 中级（Intermediate） |
| 总时长 | 约 30 分钟 |
| 幸福力主题 | 自然探索 + 感恩与善意 |
| PERMA 连接 | P 自然之美 / E 音乐沉浸 / R 分享感恩 / M 热爱自然 / A 完成合唱 |

## 语言目标

| 类型 | 内容 |
|------|------|
| 核心词汇 | mountain, river, ground, forest, grass, lake, rock, leaves, trees |
| 句型结构 | 方位介词短语（in the mountain breeze, on the rock, next to the lake） |
| 句型 | There be 句型（隐含于歌词意境） |
| 填词难度 | 低（仅填单词，6 个空格，9 词 Word Bank） |

## 五步活动流程（含英文讲稿）

---

### 第一步：自然感知导入（5 分钟）

**目标**：激活自然词汇背景知识，建立情绪连接

#### 讲稿 & 引导

> 🎵 *[播放 Edelweiss 伴奏，音量 30%，作为背景音乐]*

> **Teacher**：*[轻声，放慢语速，眼神柔和扫视全班]*
>
> "Everyone, find a comfortable position. You can sit on your chair, or on the floor. Close your eyes. Take a deep breath in... and out."
>
> *[示范：闭眼，手放在腹部，慢吸慢呼。停顿 5 秒让学生进入状态]*
>
> "Now, I want you to imagine. You are walking in a beautiful forest. The sun is warm on your face. The air is fresh and clean. You take a step... and another step..."
>
> *[停顿 8 秒，让学生沉浸画面]*
>
> "Look around. What do you see? Tall trees reaching up to the sky. A river, singing as it flows. Soft leaves dancing in the breeze. You bend down and touch the grass — it's cool and soft."
>
> *[停顿 5 秒]*
>
> "Now, find a rock by the lake. Sit down. Look at the water. How does it make you feel?"
>
> *[停顿 10 秒]*
>
> "When you're ready, slowly open your eyes."

> **Teacher**：*[恢复正常音量，面带微笑，双手张开表示欢迎]*
>
> "Welcome back! What did you see in your forest? Raise your hand and share one thing. Just one word or a short sentence."
>
> *[指向第一个举手的学生，鼓励地点头]*

> **Student A**："I saw trees."
>
> **Teacher**：*[双手向上伸展模仿树，表情惊喜]* "Beautiful! You saw trees. Tall, strong trees!" *[在白板上写 "trees"]*

> **Student B**："I heard a river."
>
> **Teacher**：*[手臂做波浪流动]* "Yes! A singing river. What a lovely sound!" *[写 "river"]*

> **Student C**："I saw the sky."
>
> **Teacher**："The sky! Good. What color was the sky?" *[手指向上]*
>
> **Student C**："Blue."
>
> **Teacher**："A beautiful blue sky. Thank you!"

> *[收集 3-5 个学生分享，将关键词写在白板上。如果学生用中文说，引导师转成英文并写在白板上，同时微笑点头鼓励。]*

**TPR 动作指南**：
- "take a deep breath" → 手放在腹部，夸张地吸气和呼气
- "tall trees" → 双臂向上伸展，手指张开
- "river singing" → 一只手臂做波浪形流动
- "leaves dancing" → 手指在头顶飘动落下
- "grass" → 弯腰，手指向下轻触
- "rock by the lake" → 蹲下，双手画圆表示湖

**引导师备忘**：
- 第一步全程用柔和的"睡前故事"语气，不要急促
- 闭眼环节如果学生害羞，允许睁眼但保持安静
- 学生分享时，每听到一个词就做对应动作，强化身体记忆
- 如果学生说不出英文，微笑着说："That's okay! Let me help you. You mean... trees?"

---

### 第二步：词汇探索（5 分钟）

**目标**：认识 9 个自然词汇，建立词-图-音-动作四重连接

#### 讲稿 & 引导

> **Teacher**：*[点击 HTML 页面 🔍 按钮，全屏展示 Word Bank]*
>
> "Now let's meet our nature words! We have nine beautiful words today. Look at the screen. I'll say the word, you repeat after me. Ready?"

> **Teacher**：*[指着 mountain 卡片，双手从胸前向上画尖峰，身体微微后仰]* "Mountain! Moun-tain!" *[夸张地发两个音节，嘴巴张大]*
>
> **All students**："Mountain!"
>
> **Teacher**：*[保持双手尖峰姿势]* "Again! Mountain!"
>
> **All students**："Mountain!"

> **Teacher**：*[指着 river 卡片，右手臂做波浪流动，身体跟着节奏左右摇摆]* "River! Riv-er! Like the water flowing..."
>
> **All students**："River!"

> **Teacher**：*[指着 ground 卡片，蹲下，双手拍地面]* "Ground! Right under your feet — ground!"
>
> **All students**："Ground!"

> **Teacher**：*[指着 forest 卡片，双臂张开画大圆，模仿很多树]* "Forest! A big, big place with many trees — for-est!"
>
> **All students**："Forest!"

> **Teacher**：*[指着 grass 卡片，弯腰，手指向下摆动]* "Grass! Soft and green under your feet — grass!"
>
> **All students**："Grass!"

> **Teacher**：*[指着 lake 卡片，双手在胸前画大圆，表情平静]* "Lake! Calm, quiet water — lake!"
>
> **All students**："Lake!"

> **Teacher**：*[指着 rock 卡片，握拳，做坚硬的表情，肌肉绷紧]* "Rock! Hard and strong — rock!"
>
> **All students**："Rock!"

> **Teacher**：*[指着 leaves 卡片，手指从头顶飘落，身体微微下蹲]* "Leaves! Dancing in the wind — leaves!"
>
> **All students**："Leaves!"

> **Teacher**：*[指着 trees 卡片，双臂向上伸展]* "Trees! Another word for our tall friends — trees!"
>
> **All students**："Trees!"

> **Teacher**：*[拍手两下，表示切换模式，表情变得兴奋]* "Great! Now — flash card game! I do the action, YOU say the word. No thinking, just shouting! Ready?"
>
> *[快速做动作：双手尖峰]*
>
> **All students**："Mountain!"
>
> *[手臂波浪]*
>
> **All students**："River!"
>
> *[手指飘落]*
>
> **All students**："Leaves!"
>
> *[握拳坚硬]*
>
> **All students**："Rock!"
>
> *[加快速度，制造欢乐的混乱感。如果学生喊错，大笑着摇头，再做一次动作。]*

> **Teacher**：*[拍手，面带满意]* "You are AMAZING! Nine words, all in your head now!"

**TPR 动作指南**：
| 词汇 | 动作 | 表情 |
|------|------|------|
| mountain | 双手从胸前向上画尖峰，身体后仰 | 仰望，敬畏 |
| river | 右臂波浪流动，身体摇摆 | 轻松，流动感 |
| ground | 弯腰，双手拍地面 | 坚定，踏实 |
| forest | 双臂张开画大圆 | 广阔，惊叹 |
| grass | 弯腰，手指向下摆动 | 柔软，温柔 |
| lake | 双手在胸前画圆 | 平静，安宁 |
| rock | 握拳，肌肉绷紧 | 坚硬，有力 |
| leaves | 手指从头顶飘落，身体下蹲 | 轻盈，飘逸 |
| trees | 双臂向上伸展，手指张开 | 挺拔，高大 |

**发音重点**：
- **mountain**：/ˈmaʊn.tən/ — "tən" 轻读，舌尖顶上颚
- **river**：/ˈrɪv.ər/ — 注意 /ɪ/ 短音，不要读成 "reever"
- **forest**：/ˈfɔːr.ɪst/ — 两个音节，重音在前
- **leaves**：/liːvz/ — 长音 /iː/，结尾 /vz/ 要振动声带

**引导师备忘**：
- 每个词做动作时保持 2 秒，让学生看清并模仿
- 闪卡游戏阶段逐渐加速，制造"来不及想只能脱口而出"的效果
- 如果有学生发音不准，不要停下来纠正，在下一轮集体朗读时自己大声示范正确发音

### 第三步：填词挑战（8 分钟）

**目标**：将词汇填入歌词，理解方位介词在语境中的使用

#### 讲稿 & 引导

> **Teacher**：*[关闭全屏 Word Bank，回到 HTML 主页，指着歌词]*
>
> "Now, look at our song. We have a beautiful melody — Edelweiss — and our own words, 'Green In My Heart.'"
>
> "See these empty spaces? *[指着第一个空格]* We need to fill them! Let's read the song together first. I'll read, you follow."

> **Teacher**：*[指着第一行，缓慢朗读，手指跟着文字移动]*
>
> "Near and far, soft and light... *[手在眼前画远近，然后做柔软的手势]*"
>
> "Dancing... hmm... something... in the mountain breeze. *[耸肩，表示不知道，手指飘落暗示]*"
>
> "What dances in the mountain breeze? What goes swish-swish in the wind?"
>
> *[等待 3 秒，看是否有学生举手]*

> **Student**："Leaves!"
>
> **Teacher**：*[眼睛睁大，惊喜]* "YES! Leaves! Dancing leaves in the mountain breeze!" *[手指飘落]* "That makes sense, right? Leaves dance in the wind!"

> **Teacher**："Now, you and your partner — open the web page on your tablet. *[示意两人一组]* You will see the words on the right side. Drag the words to the blanks. Work together! Talk to each other! Four minutes. Go!"

> *[学生在平板上拖拽填词，引导师巡视，弯腰观察每组进度，不直接给答案，而是用动作提示]*

> **Teacher**：*[巡视时对卡住的小组]* "Hmm, let's look at this line: '______ sings and the ______ can hear.' What sings? *[做手臂波浪动作]* Think about what makes a sound in nature..."

> *[4 分钟后]*

> **Teacher**：*[拍手召集]* "Alright! Let's check together. Line by line. Line two — what did you put?"

> **Student**："Leaves!"

> **Teacher**："Perfect! 'Dancing leaves in the mountain breeze.' *[手指飘落]* Now line four — this one is tricky! It has TWO blanks. What sings?"

> **Student**："River!"

> **Teacher**：*[手臂波浪]* "The river sings! And who can hear the river?"

> **Student**："Trees!"

> **Teacher**："The trees can hear! 'River sings and the trees can hear.' Some of you might have a different answer — that's okay too! Maybe the forest can hear, or the mountain can hear. There's no single right answer — as long as it makes sense in nature!"

> **Teacher**："Line five: 'In the ______ I walk and dream.' Where do you walk and dream?"

> **Student**："Forest!"

> **Teacher**："In the forest! *[双臂张开画大圆]* 'In the forest I walk and dream.' Notice the word 'in' — in the forest. That's a preposition — it tells us WHERE."

> **Teacher**："Line six: 'On the ______, next to the ______.' Two prepositions here — on and next to. What did you choose?"

> **Student**："Rock and lake!"

> **Teacher**："On the rock, next to the lake. *[蹲下，一手握拳表示石头，一手画圆表示湖]* Beautiful! You're sitting on a rock, right next to a calm lake."

> **Teacher**：*[指着白板上的方位介词]* "Let's look at our prepositions today: IN the breeze, IN the forest, ON the rock, NEXT TO the lake. These little words — in, on, next to — they help us paint the picture!"

**歌词答案**：

| 行 | 歌词 | 填空答案 | 方位介词 |
|----|------|---------|---------|
| L2 | Dancing ______ in the mountain breeze | leaves | in |
| L4 | ______ sings and the ______ can hear | river / trees | — |
| L5 | In the ______ I walk and dream | forest | in |
| L6 | On the ______, next to the ______ | rock / lake | on, next to |

**干扰词**：mountain, ground, grass（不在歌词中）

**引导师备忘**：
- L4 有两个答案（river/trees），都正确，鼓励学生解释为什么选这个词
- 如果学生用干扰词填了（如 "Dancing mountain in the breeze"），微笑说："That's creative! But does a mountain dance? Let's think what really dances in the wind..."
- 方位介词部分，用手指动作配合：in → 拳头包住手指，on → 手掌平放，next to → 双手并排
- 3 个干扰词可作为拓展："Where could we use 'mountain' in this song? Maybe: 'Near the mountain, by the trees'?"

### 第四步：乐器配器（5 分钟）

**目标**：通过乐器选择表达对自然声音的理解，发展音乐感知力

#### 讲稿 & 引导

> **Teacher**：*[向下滚动页面，展示乐器区]*
>
> "Now, let's add MUSIC to our song! Look at these eight instruments. *[指着每个乐器]* Maracas, tambourine, hand drum, triangle, ukulele, castanets, shaker, xylophone."
>
> "Each instrument makes a different sound. Listen..."

> *[如果有实物乐器，逐个演示。如果没有，用嘴模仿声音：]*

> **Teacher**："Shaker — *[模仿沙沙声]* shh-shh-shh-shh. What does that sound like in nature?"

> **Student**："Wind? Leaves?"

> **Teacher**："Yes! Wind through the leaves, or rain falling. *[手指飘落]*"

> **Teacher**："Triangle — *[模仿叮咚声]* ting-ting-ting. What in nature sounds like that?"

> **Student**："Water drops?"

> **Teacher**："Beautiful! Water drops, or a small stream. *[手臂波浪]*"

> **Teacher**："Hand drum — *[模仿咚咚声]* boom-boom. What sounds like that?"

> **Student**："Rocks falling? Thunder?"

> **Teacher**："Great ideas! Deep, heavy sounds."

> **Teacher**："Now, with your partner, choose TWO instruments for each line of the song. Think: what natural sound is in this line? Which instrument matches that sound?"

> *[示意学生操作：拖拽乐器到歌词末尾圆圈]*

> *[3 分钟后]*

> **Teacher**："Let's hear some ideas! Group one — what did you choose for 'Dancing leaves in the mountain breeze'?"

> **Student**："Shaker and tambourine!"

> **Teacher**：*[惊喜]* "Shaker for the leaves rustling, tambourine for the wind! That's so thoughtful! Why did you choose those?"

> **Student**："Because shaker sounds like leaves, and tambourine sounds like wind."

> **Teacher**："I love that! *[转向全班]* Anyone chose something different? No wrong answers here — every choice tells a story!"

**自然声音-乐器联想**：

| 自然声音 | 联想乐器 | 模仿拟声词 | 动作 |
|---------|---------|-----------|------|
| 树叶沙沙声 | 沙锤 / 砂槌 | shh-shh-shh | 双手搓动 |
| 溪水流淌声 | 三角铁 | ting-ting-ting | 手指轻弹 |
| 风吹过树林 | 铃鼓 | shh-la-la | 手腕抖动 |
| 石头滚落声 | 手鼓 | boom-boom | 手掌拍击 |
| 鸟鸣声 | 木琴 | doo-dee-doo | 手指跳跃 |
| 雨滴声 | 响板 | click-click | 拇指食指捏合 |
| 心跳声 | 手鼓 | thump-thump | 手放在胸口 |

**引导师备忘**：
- 没有标准答案！任何合理的联想都值得鼓励
- 重点不是"对不对"，而是"为什么这样选"
- 用拟声词帮助学生建立声音联想，比直接说"这个适合"更有效
- 如果学生选择困难，提示："Close your eyes, listen to this line in your head. What sound do you hear?"

---

### 第五步：合唱与表达（5 分钟）

**目标**：完整演唱歌曲，连接自然之美与内心感恩

#### 讲稿 & 引导

> **Teacher**：*[拍手，示意全班起立]*
>
> "Everyone, stand up! Let's make a big circle. *[张开双臂指挥]* Come on, bring your tablets or just your voices!"

> *[学生围成圆圈，引导师站在圆圈中]*

> **Teacher**：*[降低音量，变得柔和]* "We're going to sing our song three times. First time — no voice, just lips. Silent singing. Feel the melody in your body. Ready?"

> *[播放伴奏，音量 60%。引导师夸张地做口型，手放在胸口表示感受。全班默唱。]*

> **Teacher**：*[伴奏结束，微笑]* "Beautiful. Did you feel it? The melody is so gentle, like the forest itself."

> **Teacher**："Second time — sing out loud! All together! Follow my hands for the rhythm. *[举起双手，准备指挥]*"

> *[播放伴奏，引导师指挥节奏，用口型带领歌词。全班齐唱。]*

> **Teacher**：*[齐唱结束，双手鼓掌]* "YES! That was wonderful! Did you hear yourselves? You sound like a real choir!"

> **Teacher**："Third time — this time, add your instruments! *[示意学生]* Use your hands, tap on your legs, make the sounds you chose. Ready?"

> *[播放伴奏，全班齐唱 + 身体打击乐。引导师加入，用手拍腿模仿手鼓，打响指模仿三角铁。]*

> *[歌曲结束，保持安静 3 秒，让余韵停留]*

> **Teacher**：*[轻声，手放在胸口]* "The song says: 'Green and warm, in my heart. All this love is where we are.'"

> *[停顿 3 秒]*

> **Teacher**："I want you to think... What does 'green in my heart' mean to you? When you go outside, when you see trees and rivers and mountains — how do you feel? You can say it in English, or Chinese, or both. Just share from your heart."

> *[等待 5 秒，让学生思考]*

> **Student**："It makes me feel... happy. And calm."

> **Teacher**：*[点头，温柔微笑]* "Happy and calm. Nature gives us that, doesn't it? *[手放在胸口]* Green in your heart."

> **Student**："I want to say thank you to nature. For the trees and the air."

> **Teacher**：*[表情温暖]* "Thank you for sharing that. 'All this love is where we are' — the love is right here, right now, in this forest, in this circle, in our hearts."

> **Teacher**：*[提高音量，回到正常语气]* "Before we finish — one last thing. If you could say 'thank you' to nature for one thing, what would it be? Just one word. Shout it out!"

> **All students**：*[此起彼伏]* "Trees!" "Water!" "Air!" "Sun!" "Mountains!" "Rivers!"

> **Teacher**：*[双手张开，接收所有声音]* "Yes! Yes! All of that! Nature gives us everything. And when we sing, when we remember, that green stays in our heart. Thank you, everyone. You were amazing today!"

**引导师备忘**：
- 三遍演唱的递进：默唱（感受）→ 齐唱（表达）→ 加乐器（创造）
- 默唱环节保持安静，只做口型，让旋律在身体里流动
- 齐唱时不要纠正发音或音准，重点是情感连接
- 最后的分享环节，允许学生用中英混合表达，引导师帮他们转成简短的英文
- 如果学生不想分享，不强迫。可以说："That's okay. Just hold the feeling in your heart."
- 结尾的"Thank you shout-out"环节要快节奏、高能量，制造欢乐的收尾

## 拓展建议

1. **写作延伸**：学生仿写一段歌词，用上 3 个干扰词（mountain / ground / grass）
2. **艺术延伸**：画出"我心中的绿色"——你想象的森林/湖泊/山脉
3. **户外延伸**：带学生到户外，找到歌词中的 9 个自然元素并拍照
