# Something Found · 教学教案

## 教案元信息

| 字段 | 内容 |
|------|------|
| 绘本名称 | Something Found |
| 适配主题 | 人际关系 + 成长与变化 |
| 语言目标 | 词汇：ball, doll, boat, car, box, cap, map |
|  | 句型：Do you have…? / Where is…? / Is it…? + Yes, I do. / No, it isn't. |
| 艺术形式 | 综合材料（彩笔 + 水彩 + 拼贴 + 印章） |
| 随机机制 | 掷骰子 → 6 个点数对应 6 个藏宝位置，每人掷出不同位置 |
| 建议时长 | 30-40 min |
| 适用年龄段 | 7-12 岁（分龄调整见附录） |
| 课前准备 | 见材料清单 ↓ |

---

## 材料与准备

### 材料清单

| 类型 | 材料 | 数量 | 备注 |
|------|------|------|------|
| 每人一份 | A3 画纸 | 1 张/人 | 白色或浅色底，画藏宝地图用 |
| 每人一份 | 彩笔/蜡笔 | 1 盒/人 | 画地图 |
| 每人一份 | 水彩 | 1 套/人 | 可选，给地图上色 |
| 小组共用 | 拼贴材料 | 若干 | 杂志剪贴、彩纸、胶水 |
| 小组共用 | 印章/印泥 | 1-2 套 | 标记宝藏位置，也可用贴纸替代 |
| 教师预制 | 藏宝骰子 | 1 个/组 | 课前制作，见下方 |

### 骰子预制说明

制作一个 6 面骰子，每面写一个藏宝位置（英文 + 小图标辅助理解）：

| 面 | 内容 | 英文 |
|----|------|------|
| 1 | 📦 盒子 | in the box |
| 2 | 🗺️ 地图 | on the map |
| 3 | 🧢 帽子 | under the cap |
| 4 | ⛵ 船 | in the boat |
| 5 | 🚗 车 | on the car |
| 6 | 🪆 娃娃 | under the doll |

制作方式：
- 用硬纸板折叠成正方体，或购买空白骰子
- 每面手写英文 + 简笔画图标
- 或用打印贴纸、胶带贴在骰子上
- 确保文字清晰可读，图标辅助理解

### 环境准备

- 桌面：每组一张大桌，材料放在中央，孩子围坐
- 展示区：一面空墙或软木板，作品完成后贴上去
- 背景音乐：可选轻快探险风格（如海盗主题轻音乐），创作环节播放

### 黑板准备

课前在黑板上写好句型支架（语言输出环节用）：

```
═════════════════════════════════
     Share your treasure!
═════════════════════════════════

  Do you have a ________?
  → Yes, I do! / No, I don't.

  Where is the ________?
  → It's in / on / under the ________.

  Is it ________ the ________?
  → Yes, it is. / No, it isn't.

  Words: ball  doll  boat  car
         box  cap  map
═════════════════════════════════
```

## 分环节教学引导

### ❶ 情境引入 · 6-8 min

情境引入分三步：预热（未翻书）→ 封面揭晓 → P1-P2 共读。

---

#### 1. 预热（未翻书）· 2-3 min

绘本还没出现。用一个**神秘触摸袋**建立"寻找"的直觉——看不见，只能摸，就像故事里丢失的东西一样。

**Step 1 — 神秘袋登场**（30 秒）
（拿出一个不透明布袋，里面装一个小物件——可以是弹珠、小车、小玩偶）
> "Look at this bag. （举起袋子，摇晃，发出声音）
>  There is something inside. But you can't see it. Not yet."

（把袋子举到每个孩子面前，让他们摸一摸外面，但不打开）
> "Something is hiding in here. You can't see it. But you can… feel it?"

**Step 2 — 摸一摸，猜一猜**（45 秒）
（选 2-3 个孩子轮流伸手进袋子摸，不拿出来，不偷看）
> "Put your hand inside. Just feel. Don't look. Don't take it out.
>  What does it feel like? Round? Hard? Soft? Small?"

（等孩子描述，不确认也不否认）
> "Interesting! What do you think it is?"

（等孩子猜。接纳所有猜测，不揭晓答案）
> "Maybe! Maybe not. We'll find out later."

（把袋子放在一旁可见但碰不到的地方）
> "The secret is still in the bag. It's… lost. For now."

**Step 3 — 悬念钩子**（30 秒）
> "You know that feeling — when something is hidden. When you can't see it.
>  When you want to find it, but you don't know where it is."

（停顿，语气放低）
> "Today, someone has lost something too. Something important.
>  They looked everywhere. But they can't find it."

（停顿）
> "What did they lose? And where could it be?"

（不等回答）
> "Let's find out."

**Step 4 — 拿出绘本**（不翻页，封面朝自己）
> "I have a story to show you. It's about something lost. And something found."

（把绘本封面朝向自己，不让孩子看到）
> "Are you ready to help them find it?"

**备选话术**

```
【如果孩子不敢摸】
  "That's okay. You can just watch. （让下一个孩子摸）
   Tell us — does it look like they found something hard or soft?"

【如果孩子一摸就猜对了】
  "Ooh, maybe! （不确认，眨眼）But we'll keep the secret a little longer.
   Let's see if the story has the same secret… or a different one."

【如果孩子说"让我看！"】
  "Not yet! The secret is still hiding. Just like the thing in our story.
   First, let's help someone else find what THEY lost."
```

---

#### 2. 封面揭晓 · 1 min

第一次展示绘本封面。

**Step 1 — 揭晓**
（缓慢翻过绘本，展示封面）
> "This is our story. It's called…"

（指向封面标题）
> "Something Found."

**Step 2 — 观察封面**（30 秒，不翻页）
> "Look at the cover. Something Found.
>  What do you think was lost? What do you think they found?"

（等孩子回应，接纳所有猜测）
> "Maybe a toy? Maybe a friend? Let's find out."

**Step 3 — 过渡到 P1**
> "Ready? Let's open the book…"

（缓慢翻开第一页）

---

#### 3. P1-P2 共读 · 3-4 min

---

**【P1】绘本文字：**
Someone has lost something. They looked under the bed, behind the door, inside the box. But they cannot find it.

#### 氛围营造

| 维度 | 内容 |
|------|------|
| 情绪基调 | 好奇 + 一点点担心 + 想帮忙 |
| 语气 | 放慢，像在讲一个谜，每个"找"的动作都稍作停顿 |
| 肢体 | 每读一个位置就做对应动作（弯腰、转身、打开），孩子会跟着做 |
| 空间 | 孩子围坐半圈，能看清绘本画面，也能看到彼此的脸 |

#### 分步话术

**Step 1 — 读**（先完整读完，不打断，语速慢，配合动作）
> "Someone has lost something."

（停顿）
> "They looked under the bed. （弯腰看）
>  Behind the door. （转身看）
>  Inside the box. （做打开动作）
>  But they cannot find it."

（读完合上绘本，停顿 3 秒，给孩子消化画面）

**Step 2 — 观察**（指向画面，邀请孩子看）
> "Look at the picture. Where did they look? Point to the places."

（等孩子指——bed, door, box）

**Step 3 — 提问**（把观察引向情绪）
> "They looked everywhere. How do you think they feel?"

（如果孩子沉默，给选项）
> "Happy? Or worried?"

**Step 4 — 情绪锚点**（帮孩子命名情绪，建立共情）
> "Worried. Yes. When you lose something you love, you feel worried.
>  Have you ever felt worried like that?"

（等 1-2 个孩子简短回应，不做长篇讨论）

**Step 5 — 铺垫悬念**（为 P2 埋钩子）
> "They looked under the bed. Behind the door. Inside the box.
>  But they still cannot find it. Not yet."

（语气放低，留悬念）

#### 备选话术

```
【如果孩子完全不回应】
  "That's okay. Let's look together."
  （指向画面具体元素）
  "I see a bed. What else do you see? Just point."
  （孩子指了 → 老师说）"Yes! A box. Good eyes!"

【如果孩子只说中文】
  "Ah, 床！Yes, a bed! In English, we say 'bed.'
   Can you say it with me? Bed!"
  （接纳中文，自然带出英文）

【如果孩子太兴奋，七嘴八舌收不住】
  "Wow, you have so many ideas! Let's save some for later.
   （合上绘本一半）Now, shhh… let's turn the page quietly."
```

#### 接话策略

| 孩子可能说 | 老师怎么接 |
|-----------|-----------|
| "Sad!" | "Yes, a little sad. They lost something special. Let's see if we can help." |
| "Under the bed!" | "Yes! They looked under the bed. But it wasn't there. Where else?" |
| "Worried!" | "Worried! Good word. They don't know where it is. That's a worrying feeling." |
| "I don't know." | "That's okay! Let's look at the picture together. See their face? What do you see?" |
| "床！" / "盒子！"（描述画面元素） | "Yes! A bed! And a box. Good eyes! Now, look at the person. How do they feel?" |

---

**【P2】绘本文字：**
They look a little sad. Will you help them?

#### 过渡

> » TRANSITION（P1 → P2）
> "Now, let's turn the page. Ready? Quietly…"

#### 分步话术

**Step 1 — 读**（语气温和，像在询问）
> "They look a little sad."

（停顿 2 秒，让孩子看角色的表情）
> "Will you help them?"

（停顿，等孩子回应。通常会点头或说 yes）

**Step 2 — 确认回应**（如果孩子点头了）
> "Yes? You will help? Good! You are kind helpers."

（如果孩子没反应）
> "I think we can help. Let's help them together."

**Step 3 — 铺垫任务**
> "They need us. Let's go on a treasure hunt!"

（语气从温柔转向轻快，为 P3 做铺垫）

---

### ❷ 任务驱动（P3）· 1-2 min

---

**【P3】绘本文字：**
Let's go on a treasure hunt. We will look high and low, near and far. We will find what is lost — together.

#### 过渡

> » TRANSITION（P2 → P3）
> "You said yes! So let's go. We are treasure hunters now."

#### 分步话术

**Step 1 — 读**（语气轻快，像在发起冒险）
> "Let's go on a treasure hunt.
>  We will look high and low, near and far.
>  We will find what is lost — together."

（读完停顿 2 秒）

**Step 2 — 确认任务**（用孩子的话确认）
> "So… what are we going to do?"

（等孩子回应。可能的回答：find treasure / help them / look for things）
> "Yes! We are going to find the treasure. Together!"

**Step 3 — 角色代入**（"我们"一起做）
> "Are you ready to be treasure hunters? You and me, together?"

（点头，用表情邀请）
> "Yes? Let's go!"

**Step 4 — 保留悬念**（不展开怎么做）
> "But first… we need to know WHERE to look."

（神秘地拿出骰子，但不展示内容）
> "Look what I have. This will help us."

#### 备选话术

```
【如果孩子说"不会找"】
  "Don't worry. I have a special tool. It will tell us where to look!"

【如果孩子急于知道怎么做】
  "Wait, wait! Not yet. First, let me show you this."
  （举起骰子，摇晃）"Hear that? It's a secret weapon."
```

### ❸ 创作引导（P4-P5）★ 核心 · 15-20 min

---

#### 3.1 随机机制操作（P4）· 5-8 min

**【P4】绘本文字：**
🔮 Roll the dice. Where will your treasure hide? One number — one secret place.

##### 机制说明

掷一个 6 面骰子，每面对应一个藏宝位置。每个孩子掷一次，掷出的位置不同 → 每个人的藏宝地图天然个性化。隐藏位置包括：in the box, on the map, under the cap, in the boat, on the car, under the doll。

##### 过渡

> » TRANSITION（P3 → P4）
> （举起骰子，摇晃）
> "This is our treasure dice. Each side tells us a secret place.
>  Where will YOUR treasure hide? Let's find out!"

##### 分步操作

**Step 1 — 读 P4 文字**
> "Roll the dice. Where will your treasure hide?
>  One number — one secret place."

（语气神秘，像在宣布游戏规则）

**Step 2 — 示范掷骰子**（老师先做一次）
> "Let me show you first. I'll roll the dice."

（掷骰子，让大家看到结果，比如掷到"in the box"）
> "Look! In the box! My treasure hides in the box."

（指骰子上的文字）
> "In the box. Say it with me: in the box!"

**Step 3 — 依次掷骰子**（每人掷一次）
> "Now it's your turn! Who wants to go first?"

（孩子轮流掷骰子。每次掷完：）
> "What did you get? Read it!"

（帮孩子读出位置）
> "On the map! Your treasure is on the map. Great!"

（对每个孩子——不比较，每个结果都是"特别的"）
> "Under the cap! That's a tricky hiding place."
> "In the boat! Your treasure can sail away."
> "On the car! Your treasure goes for a ride."
> "Under the doll! Very sneaky!"
> "In the box! A classic hiding spot."

**Step 4 — 总结每个人的位置**
> "Let's see — （指一圈孩子）
>  You have 'in the box,' you have 'on the map,'
>  you have 'under the cap'…
>  Everyone has a different hiding place! That's perfect.
>  Each treasure map will be different. Just like you."

##### 悬念营造要点

- 掷骰子前摇晃，发出声音制造期待
- 骰子落地后停顿 2 秒，让孩子自己看，不抢答
- 每个结果都给予积极回应，不比较"哪个位置更好"
- 掷骰子时让其他孩子一起倒数 "3… 2… 1…"

##### 个性化强调话术

- "Your hiding place is different from your friend's. That's what makes it special!"
- "There's no 'best' hiding place. YOUR place is the right one."
- "I love how everyone has a different secret. Don't tell anyone yet!"
- "Shh… your treasure's hiding place is YOUR secret. For now."

#### 3.2 创作执行（P5）· 10-15 min

**【P5】绘本文字：**
Now you know. Draw your treasure map. Mark the spot. Shh… don't tell anyone yet!

##### 过渡

> » TRANSITION（P4 → P5）
> "Now you know your secret hiding place. Time to make a treasure map!"

##### 分步操作

**Step 1 — 读 P5 文字**
> "Now you know. Draw your treasure map. Mark the spot.
>  Shh… don't tell anyone yet!"

（读"Shh…"时把食指放在嘴唇上，语气像在分享秘密）

**Step 2 — 材料分发**
> "Here is your paper."

（每人发一张 A3 画纸）
> "And here are your materials — crayons, watercolors, and collage pieces.
>  You can use anything you want. Take what you need."

**Step 3 — 创作引导语**（完整英文，老师直接念）
> "Now, take a moment. Think about your treasure map.
>  What does it look like? Is there a road? A river? A mountain?
>  Where is your hiding place? Remember — it's a SECRET.
>  Draw your map. Use colors. Use collage. Use stamps.
>  Mark the spot with an X — or a stamp — or a star.
>  It's YOUR treasure map. There is no wrong way.
>  Ready? Begin!"

**Step 4 — 巡场**

✓ 用提问代替指导：
- "Tell me about your map. What's this part?"
- "Where is your treasure hiding? Don't tell me — just point to the spot on your map."
- "What made you choose this color?"
- "Oh, I see a road! Where does it lead?"

✓ 遇到"我不会画"时：
- "Just start with one thing. Draw a line. That can be a road."
- "Use a stamp! Put a stamp on the paper. That's your starting point."
- （不给示范，不给范例图。不给"正确答案"）

✓ 遇到互相比较时：
- "Everyone's map is different. That's what makes it special. Let me see YOURS."
- "Your map is for YOUR treasure. It doesn't have to look like anyone else's."

✓ 遇到"可以画真的东西吗"（车、船等）：
- "Yes! You can draw anything. A car, a boat, a mountain, a forest. It's YOUR map."

**巡场英文话术速查**

| 场景 | 话术 |
|------|------|
| 孩子卡住不动 | "Just start with one line. A road. A river. Anything." |
| 孩子问"这样对吗" | "There's no right or wrong. If you like it, it's right." |
| 孩子画得很快 | "What else can you add? Trees? A secret path? A compass?" |
| 孩子只用一种颜色 | "What if you add another color? Try red for the X." |
| 孩子模仿别人 | "That looks like your friend's map. What would YOU like to add?" |
| 孩子想说出藏宝位置 | "Shh! Keep it a secret! Don't tell anyone yet." |

**Step 5 — 收尾信号**

提前 2 分钟：
> "We have about 2 more minutes.
>  What's the last thing you want to add to your treasure map?
>  And don't forget — mark the spot with an X or a stamp!"

时间到：
> "Okay, treasure hunters, hands up! （举手，等孩子看过来）
>  Maps down. Let's look at what we made."

### ❹ 语言输出（P6）· 5-8 min

---

**【P6】绘本文字：**
Now, share your treasure!

💬 "Do you have a ball?" "Yes, I do!"
💬 "Where is the doll?" "It's in the box."
💬 "Is it under the cap?" "No, it isn't."
💬 "Look! The car is on the map, and the boat is here too."

#### 过渡

> » TRANSITION（P5 → P6）
> （举起一个孩子的藏宝地图，展示给大家）
> "Look at these treasure maps! Now it's time to share the secrets.
>  Let's talk about our treasures."

#### 黑板句型支架

（课前已写好，此时指向黑板）

```
═════════════════════════════════
     Share your treasure!
═════════════════════════════════

  Do you have a ________?
  → Yes, I do! / No, I don't.

  Where is the ________?
  → It's in / on / under the ________.

  Is it ________ the ________?
  → Yes, it is. / No, it isn't.

  Words: ball  doll  boat  car
         box  cap  map
═════════════════════════════════
```

#### 分步话术

**Step 1 — 读标题句**
> "Now, share your treasure!"

（指向黑板上的句型提示）

**Step 2 — 示范**（老师先做，选一个孩子的作品）
> "Let me try first."

（走到一个孩子旁边，看他的地图）
> "Hmm… I wonder what's on your map.
>  Do you have a ball?"

（孩子回答 Yes/No）
> "Where is the doll?"

（指地图上的某个位置）
> "Is it in the box?"

（孩子回答）
> "Wow! Your treasure map is full of secrets!"

**Step 3 — 配对练习**
> "Now, find a partner. Show your partner your treasure map.
>  Ask each other questions. Use the words on the board."

（指黑板句型提示）
> "Ready? Go!"

（巡场 2-3 分钟，倾听，记录 1-2 个典型表达）

**Step 4 — 自愿展示**（选 1-2 组）
> "Who wants to share their treasure with everyone?"

（展示者描述地图，其他孩子可以提问："Is it under the cap?" "Do you have a boat?"）

**Step 5 — 收束到板书**
> "Wow, so many treasures! So many different maps.
>  Let's look at the words we used today."

（快速过一遍黑板词汇）

#### 备选话术

```
【如果孩子只说单词，说不出完整句子】
  "A ball! Yes! Can you say 'I have a ball'? Let's say it together."
  （老师领读，孩子跟读）

【如果孩子不愿意说】
  "That's okay. You can just point. What's this? （指地图上的元素）
   A boat! Yes. （老师帮他说完整）There is a boat on your map. Good!"

【如果配对练习时两个孩子都不说话】
  "Let me help you start. （拿一个孩子的作品）
   Ask your partner: 'Do you have a car?'
   （让另一个孩子指）Point to your map. Good! Now answer."
```

#### 纠错策略

- 不打断流利表达
- 用自然复述纠正：
  - 孩子："The ball is in box."
  - 老师："Yes! The ball is in the box. Good!"
- 严重错误课后单独纠正，不在全班面前纠正

### ❺ 情感收束（P7）· 2-3 min

---

**【P7】绘本文字：**
You found it. Not just a toy. You found a friend to share with. And that is the best treasure of all.

#### 过渡

> » TRANSITION（P6 → P7）
> （语气放慢，音量降低，从热闹回到安静）
