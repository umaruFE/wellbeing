# A Seat for You · 教学教案

## 教案元信息

| 字段 | 内容 |
|------|------|
| 绘本名称 | A Seat for You |
| 适配主题 | 家庭与归属 + 感恩与善意 |
| 语言目标 | 词汇：breakfast, lunch, dinner, noodles, cake, bread, egg, juice, milk, soup, fruit, rice, meat, vegetables |
|  | 句型：I'd like some… / Have some… / Would you like some…? / Yes, please. / No, thank you. |
| 艺术形式 | 综合材料（黏土 + 水彩 + 拼贴 + 回收材料） |
| 随机机制 | 抽食物卡片 → 每人抽 3 张，翻牌揭晓，组合方式千人千面 |
| 建议时长 | 30-40 min |
| 适用年龄段 | 7-12 岁（分龄调整见附录） |
| 课前准备 | 见材料清单 ↓ |

---

## 材料与准备

### 材料清单

| 类型 | 材料 | 数量 | 备注 |
|------|------|------|------|
| 每人一份 | A3 画纸 | 1 张/人 | 白色底，画餐盘用 |
| 每人一份 | 黏土/橡皮泥 | 1 套/人 | 捏食物造型 |
| 每人一份 | 水彩 | 1 套/人 | 画餐盘和食物 |
| 每人一份 | 彩纸 + 胶水 | 1 套/人 | 拼贴食物 |
| 小组共用 | 回收瓶盖 | 若干 | 当盘子/碗 |
| 教师预制 | 食物卡片 | 14 张/组 | 课前制作，见下方 |

### 食物卡片预制说明

制作 14 张卡片，每张写一个食物词（英文 + 简笔画图标辅助理解），背面统一朝下：

| 编号 | 食物 | 英文 | 图标建议 |
|------|------|------|---------|
| 1 | 面条 | noodles | 🍜 |
| 2 | 蛋糕 | cake | 🎂 |
| 3 | 面包 | bread | 🍞 |
| 4 | 鸡蛋 | egg | 🥚 |
| 5 | 果汁 | juice | 🧃 |
| 6 | 牛奶 | milk | 🥛 |
| 7 | 汤 | soup | 🍲 |
| 8 | 水果 | fruit | 🍎 |
| 9 | 米饭 | rice | 🍚 |
| 10 | 肉 | meat | 🥩 |
| 11 | 蔬菜 | vegetables | 🥕 |
| 12 | 早餐 | breakfast | 🌅 |
| 13 | 午餐 | lunch | ☀️ |
| 14 | 晚餐 | dinner | 🌙 |

制作方式：
- 用硬卡纸裁成 8×10cm 卡片
- 正面手写英文 + 简笔画图标
- 背面统一颜色（不透露内容）
- 放入一个神秘盒子或布袋中，抽取时更有仪式感

### 环境准备

- 桌面：每组一张大桌，材料放在中央，孩子围坐（像围坐在一张大餐桌旁）
- 展示区：一面空墙或软木板，"餐盘"作品完成后贴上去，形成"班级大餐桌"
- 背景音乐：可选温馨家庭餐桌氛围音乐（轻柔的吉他或钢琴），创作环节播放

### 黑板准备

课前在黑板上写好句型支架（语言输出环节用）：

```
═════════════════════════════════
      Share your meal!
═════════════════════════════════

  I'd like some ________.
  → Have some ________!

  Would you like some ________?
  → Yes, please! / No, thank you.

  Try the ________!
  → Mmm, and some ________!

  Words: noodles  cake  bread  egg
         juice  milk  soup  fruit
         rice  meat  vegetables
         breakfast  lunch  dinner
═════════════════════════════════
```

## 分环节教学引导

### ❶ 情境引入 · 6-8 min

情境引入分三步：预热（未翻书）→ 封面揭晓 → P1-P2 共读。

---

#### 1. 预热（未翻书）· 2-3 min

绘本还没出现。用一张**真实的空椅子**建立"陪伴"和"邀请"的视觉钩子——不需要想象，孩子用眼睛就能看见那个空缺。

**Step 1 — 空椅子登场**（30 秒）
（课前在教室中央放一把空椅子，孩子围坐时能看到它）
> "Look around. （慢慢扫视一圈孩子，然后目光停在空椅子上）
>  Do you see that chair?"

（指向空椅子，停顿 3 秒，让孩子自己注视）
> "It's empty. There's no one sitting there. Not yet."

（走近椅子，轻轻碰一下椅背）
> "Every chair is waiting for someone. Who could sit here?"

**Step 2 — 邀请想象**（30 秒）
> "Think for a moment. If you could invite anyone to sit in this chair —
>  anyone in the whole world — who would you choose?"

（等 5 秒，不点名，让孩子自己想）
> "Maybe your mom. Maybe your best friend. Maybe someone you haven't seen
>  for a long time. Maybe someone who's lonely."

（停顿，语气放轻）
> "Now imagine — this chair is at a dinner table. And the table is full of food.
>  But the chair is still empty. No one is sitting there."

**Step 3 — 悬念钩子**（30 秒）
> "Today, there is a table like that. Full of food.
>  But the person at the table is eating alone. They have a seat.
>  An empty seat. Right next to them. And they are waiting."

（停顿，语气放低）
> "Who is that seat for?"

（不等回答，制造悬念）
> "Let's find out."

**Step 4 — 拿出绘本**（不翻页，封面朝自己）
> "I have a story to show you. It's about a table. And a seat. And someone
>  who doesn't want to eat alone."

（把绘本封面朝向自己，不让孩子看到）
> "Are you ready to sit at this table?"

**备选话术**

```
【如果孩子一直盯着空椅子，注意力分散】
  "I see you looking at that chair. It's a special chair, isn't it?
   Let's keep it in our minds. We'll come back to it later."

【如果孩子说"没人坐"或"空的"】
  "Yes, it's empty. But not for long. In our story, that seat is for someone.
   Someone special. Let's find out who."

【如果孩子说"我想坐！"】
  （笑）"Maybe you can! But first — let's find out who the seat in our
   story is for. Then we'll see about this one."
```

---

#### 2. 封面揭晓 · 1 min

第一次展示绘本封面。

**Step 1 — 揭晓**
（缓慢翻过绘本，展示封面）
> "This is our story. It's called…"

（指向封面标题）
> "A Seat for You."

**Step 2 — 观察封面**（30 秒，不翻页）
> "Look at the cover. A Seat for You.
>  What do you see? A table? A chair? Food?"

（等孩子回应）
> "Who do you think this seat is for?"

（接纳所有猜测，不揭示答案）
> "Maybe it's for you! Let's find out."

**Step 3 — 过渡到 P1**
> "Ready? Let's open the book…"

（缓慢翻开第一页）

---

#### 3. P1-P2 共读 · 3-4 min

---

**【P1】绘本文字：**
Someone is hungry. Breakfast, lunch, or dinner — every meal is a reason to come together.

#### 氛围营造

| 维度 | 内容 |
|------|------|
| 情绪基调 | 温暖 + 一点点孤单 + 期待 |
| 语气 | 缓慢、温暖，像在讲一个关于家的故事 |
| 肢体 | 绘本捧在胸前，读到 meal 相关词时做温和手势（捧碗、举杯） |
| 空间 | 孩子围坐半圈，像围坐在一张大餐桌旁 |

#### 分步话术

**Step 1 — 读**（先完整读完，不打断，语速慢）
> "Someone is hungry."

（停顿）
> "Breakfast, lunch, or dinner — every meal is a reason to come together."

（读完合上绘本，停顿 3 秒，给孩子消化）

**Step 2 — 观察**（指向画面，邀请孩子看）
> "Look at the picture. What meals do you see?"

（等孩子指——breakfast, lunch, dinner）
> "When do you eat breakfast? In the morning! Lunch? In the afternoon! Dinner? In the evening!"

**Step 3 — 提问**（把观察引向情绪）
> "Every meal is a reason to come together. Who do you eat with?"

（等孩子回应。可能的回答：妈妈、爸爸、朋友、一个人）
> "Eating together feels good, doesn't it?"

**Step 4 — 情绪锚点**（帮孩子建立"一起吃饭=温暖"的连接）
> "When we eat together, we share food. We share stories. We share time.
>  That's what makes a meal… a meal."

（语气温暖，不展开说教）

**Step 5 — 铺垫悬念**（为 P2 埋钩子）
> "But this someone… they are hungry. And they are eating alone.
>  They have food. But they don't have someone to share it with."

（语气放低，留悬念）

#### 备选话术

```
【如果孩子完全不回应】
  "That's okay. Let's look together."
  （指向画面具体元素）
  "I see a table. What else do you see? Just point."
  （孩子指了 → 老师说）"Yes! Food on the table. Good eyes!"

【如果孩子只说中文】
  "Ah, 早餐！Yes, breakfast! In English, we say 'breakfast.'
   Can you say it with me? Breakfast!"
  （接纳中文，自然带出英文）

【如果孩子太兴奋，七嘴八舌收不住】
  "Wow, everyone loves food! (笑) Let's save the food talk for later.
   （合上绘本一半）Now, shhh… let's turn the page quietly."
```

#### 接话策略

| 孩子可能说 | 老师怎么接 |
|-----------|-----------|
| "I eat with my mom!" | "That's lovely! Eating with mom is the best. This someone… they don't have anyone to eat with today." |
| "I like noodles!" | "Noodles! Me too! I wonder if this someone likes noodles… Let's see." |
| "Breakfast!" | "Yes! Breakfast is the first meal of the day. Do you eat breakfast with your family?" |
| "I don't know." | "That's okay! Let's look at the picture together. See the table? It's full of food. But something is missing…" |
| "吃饭！"（描述画面） | "Yes! Eating! Good. Now, look at the table. Is there anyone sitting with them?" |

---

**【P2】绘本文字：**
But they don't want to eat alone. Will you sit with them?

#### 过渡

> » TRANSITION（P1 → P2）
> "Now, let's turn the page. Ready? Quietly…"

#### 分步话术

**Step 1 — 读**（语气温和，像在邀请）
> "But they don't want to eat alone."

（停顿 2 秒，让孩子感受"alone"这个词）
> "Will you sit with them?"

（停顿，等孩子回应。通常会点头或说 yes）

**Step 2 — 确认回应**（如果孩子点头了）
> "Yes? You will sit with them? That's so kind. You are good friends."

（如果孩子没反应）
> "I think we can sit with them. Let's sit together."

**Step 3 — 铺垫任务**
> "They have an empty seat. And it's for you. Let's go fill the table!"

（语气从温柔转向温暖、轻快，为 P3 做铺垫）

---

### ❷ 任务驱动（P3）· 1-2 min

---

**【P3】绘本文字：**
Let's fill the table. Milk, juice, fruit, vegetables — so many good things to share. Your plate is waiting.

#### 过渡

> » TRANSITION（P2 → P3）
> "You said yes! Now the table is waiting for us. Let's fill it up!"

#### 分步话术

**Step 1 — 读**（语气温暖，像在描述一桌丰盛的饭菜）
> "Let's fill the table.
>  Milk, juice, fruit, vegetables — so many good things to share.
>  Your plate is waiting."

（读完停顿 2 秒）

**Step 2 — 确认任务**（用孩子的话确认）
> "So… what are we going to do?"

（等孩子回应。可能的回答：fill the table / make food / share）
> "Yes! We are going to fill the table with good food. Together!"

**Step 3 — 角色代入**（"我们"一起准备食物）
> "What would you like to put on your plate?"

（等 1-2 个孩子说）
> "Great ideas! Now, let's see what we can choose from."

**Step 4 — 保留悬念**（不展开怎么做）
> "But first… I have something special."

（神秘地拿出食物卡片盒，但不打开）
> "Inside this box are many kinds of food. You will pick your own.
>  But you can't see what's inside. Not yet!"

#### 备选话术

```
【如果孩子说"我不饿"】
  "That's okay! We're not really eating. We're making a special meal to share.
   A meal full of kindness."

【如果孩子急于知道怎么做】
  "Wait, wait! Not yet. First, let me show you this."
  （摇晃卡片盒）"Hear that? Secrets inside!"
```

### ❸ 创作引导（P4-P5）★ 核心 · 15-20 min

---

#### 3.1 随机机制操作（P4）· 5-8 min

**【P4】绘本文字：**
🎴 Pick your cards. Three cards, face down. What will be on your plate today?

##### 机制说明

14 张食物卡片，全部背面朝上放在桌子中央。每个孩子抽 3 张，翻牌揭晓。每人的 3 张组合不同 → 餐盘天然个性化。翻牌瞬间的 delight 是核心体验。

##### 过渡

> » TRANSITION（P3 → P4）
> （拿出卡片盒，放在桌子中央，所有卡片背面朝上）
> "Here are all the foods. Noodles, cake, bread, eggs, soup, rice…
>  They are all face down. You can't see what's inside.
>  Each of you will pick three cards. Three cards — your very own meal."

##### 分步操作

**Step 1 — 读 P4 文字**
> "Pick your cards. Three cards, face down.
>  What will be on your plate today?"

（语气神秘，像在宣布一个惊喜）

**Step 2 — 示范抽卡**（老师先做一次）
> "Let me show you first. I'll pick three cards."

（从卡片堆中抽出 3 张，不翻面，举起来）
> "I have three cards. But I don't know what they are yet.
>  Are you ready to see?"

（翻第一张）
> "Look! Noodles!"

（翻第二张）
> "And… eggs!"

（翻第三张）
> "And… fruit! Noodles, eggs, and fruit. That's my meal."

**Step 3 — 依次抽卡**（每人抽 3 张，不翻面）
> "Now it's your turn! Come pick three cards. Don't turn them over yet!"

（孩子轮流抽卡。抽完后每个人手里有 3 张背面朝上的卡片）
> "Everyone has three cards. Hold them tight. Don't look yet!"

（等所有人抽完）
> "Okay, are you ready? On the count of three, turn them over!
>  One… two… THREE!"

（所有人同时翻牌——这是核心惊喜时刻）
> "Wow! Look at all the different meals!
>  You have bread, juice, and meat! A big meal!
>  You have cake, fruit, and milk! A sweet meal!
>  You have noodles, eggs, and vegetables! A healthy meal!"

**Step 4 — 总结每人的餐盘**
> "Let's go around. What's on YOUR plate?"
> （每个孩子说出自己的 3 个食物词）
> "Every plate is different! That's wonderful.
>  No two meals are the same. Just like no two families are the same."

##### 悬念营造要点

- 卡片始终保持背面朝上，直到所有人抽完
- 翻牌前倒数 3-2-1，所有人同时翻——制造集体惊喜
- 每个人的组合都给予积极回应，用"big meal / sweet meal / healthy meal"等标签增加趣味
- 不比较"谁的更好"，每个组合都是"特别的"

##### 个性化强调话术

- "Your meal is different from your friend's. That's what makes it YOURS!"
- "There's no 'best' meal. YOUR meal is the right one for you."
- "I love how everyone's plate is different. Like a big family dinner!"
- "Noodles AND cake? That's a fun meal! What a great combination."

#### 3.2 创作执行（P5）· 10-15 min

**【P5】绘本文字：**
Turn them over! Draw what you got. Your plate — your story.

##### 过渡

> » TRANSITION（P4 → P5）
> "Now you know what's on your plate. Time to make it come alive!"

##### 分步操作

**Step 1 — 读 P5 文字**
> "Turn them over! Draw what you got.
>  Your plate — your story."

（语气温暖，有期待感）

**Step 2 — 材料分发**
> "Here is your paper."

（每人发一张 A3 画纸）
> "And here are your materials — clay, watercolors, colored paper, and bottle caps.
>  You can use anything you want. Take what you need."

（展示瓶盖）
> "These bottle caps can be plates! Or bowls! Or cups!"

**Step 3 — 创作引导语**（完整英文，老师直接念）
> "Now, look at your three cards. That's YOUR meal.
>  How will you make it?
>  You can draw it with watercolors. You can shape it with clay.
>  You can cut and paste with colored paper.
>  You can use bottle caps for plates.
>  Put your three foods on your plate. Make it look delicious!
>  It's YOUR plate. YOUR story. There is no wrong way.
>  Ready? Begin!"

**Step 4 — 巡场**

✓ 用提问代替指导：
- "Tell me about your plate. What's this?"
- "How did you make this? Clay? Watercolor? I love it!"
- "What's the most delicious thing on your plate?"
- "Oh, your noodles look so real! How did you make them?"

✓ 遇到"我不会做"时：
- "Just start with one thing. Make one food. Use clay — roll it, press it, shape it."
- "Use colored paper. Cut out a circle. That's a cake!"
- "Use watercolors. Paint something yellow. That could be an egg!"
- （不给示范，不给范例图。不给"正确答案"）

✓ 遇到互相比较时：
- "Everyone's plate is different. That's what makes it special. Let me see YOURS."
- "Your plate is for YOUR meal. It doesn't have to look like anyone else's."

✓ 遇到"我抽到的食物我不喜欢"：
- "That's okay! You can make it look funny. A silly noodle! A tiny cake!"
- "Or you can trade one card with a friend. But only if you both want to!"

**巡场英文话术速查**

| 场景 | 话术 |
|------|------|
| 孩子卡住不动 | "Just start with one thing. Pick one food. What's the easiest one to make?" |
| 孩子问"这样对吗" | "There's no right or wrong. If you like it, it's right." |
| 孩子做得很快 | "What else can you add? A fork? A spoon? A napkin? A cup of water?" |
| 孩子只用一种材料 | "What if you add something else? Try clay for the bread and paint for the soup." |
| 孩子模仿别人 | "That looks like your friend's plate. What would YOU like to add?" |
| 黏土太干/太硬 | "Try pressing it flat. Or add a little water. Or switch to paper!" |

**Step 5 — 收尾信号**

提前 2 分钟：
> "We have about 2 more minutes.
>  What's the last thing you want to add to your plate?
>  A drink? A dessert? A little decoration?"

时间到：
> "Okay, chefs, hands up! （举手，等孩子看过来）
>  Plates down. Let's look at our big family dinner!"

### ❹ 语言输出（P6）· 5-8 min

---

**【P6】绘本文字：**
Now, share your meal!

💬 "I'd like some noodles." "Have some noodles!"
💬 "Would you like some cake?" "Yes, please!"
💬 "No, thank you. I'd like some rice and meat."
💬 "Try the bread and egg." "Mmm, and some soup!"

#### 过渡

> » TRANSITION（P5 → P6）
> （举起一个孩子的餐盘作品，展示给大家）
> "Look at all these delicious plates! Now let's share our meals.
>  At a real dinner table, we share food. Let's share ours!"

#### 黑板句型支架

（课前已写好，此时指向黑板）

```
═════════════════════════════════
      Share your meal!
═════════════════════════════════

  I'd like some ________.
  → Have some ________!

  Would you like some ________?
  → Yes, please! / No, thank you.

  Try the ________!
  → Mmm, and some ________!

  Words: noodles  cake  bread  egg
         juice  milk  soup  fruit
         rice  meat  vegetables
         breakfast  lunch  dinner
═════════════════════════════════
```

#### 分步话术

**Step 1 — 读标题句**
> "Now, share your meal!"

（指向黑板上的句型提示）

**Step 2 — 示范**（老师先做，选一个孩子的作品）
> "Let me try first."

（走到一个孩子旁边，看他的餐盘）
> "Hmm… your plate looks delicious!
>  I'd like some noodles."

（孩子说 Have some noodles!）
> "Thank you! Would you like some cake?"

（孩子回答 Yes/No）
> "Mmm, try the bread and egg. And some soup!"

（对话自然，像真的在餐桌上）

**Step 3 — 配对练习**
> "Now, find a partner. Show your partner your plate.
>  Share your meal! Use the words on the board."

（指黑板句型提示）
> "Be kind. Say 'please' and 'thank you.' Ready? Go!"

（巡场 2-3 分钟，倾听，记录 1-2 个典型表达）

**Step 4 — 自愿展示**（选 1-2 组）
> "Who wants to share their meal with everyone?"

（展示者描述餐盘，邀请其他孩子"品尝"——"Would you like some…?"）

**Step 5 — 收束到板书**
